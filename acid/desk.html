Own the thread.

People repeat what happened to them. The queue, the tone, and the recovery are the public voice after something has already gone wrong.

  1. Today

    A person is blocked, or you were wrong, or a promise you printed is already late. Someone named on the queue answers before the day ends.

  2. The window

    Everything else waits for the reply time you publish. If that time is two business days, the queue has to make two business days true.

  3. The owner

    Every thread has a name. When that person is out, the queue shows who picked it up. No orphan threads.

  4. The tone

    First line is the fact. Same words as the public voice. If you were wrong, say so before you explain.

  5. The slip

    If the time you gave moves, you write first. The customer should not have to check whether you forgot.
