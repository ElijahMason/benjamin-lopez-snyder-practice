Read it aloud before it goes out.

A posting practice is who may speak, how often, and what stays off the public record. One person owns the last read. If they cannot say the line at a counter, it does not post.

Pick a cadence you have already kept. If one honest note a week is the true number, print that number. Leave unposted: a private complaint, a joke that needs the room, a promise the desk cannot meet.

Voice study. An exercise. No brand names.

Situation. A city library moves Tuesday hours and closes one Monday for inventory.

Stiff line: "Big news for everyone who loves the library."

Study line: "Tuesday hours move to 10 to 7 starting May 4. The desk is closed Monday that week for inventory."

Voice study. An exercise. No brand names.

Situation. A plumber missed the afternoon window.

Study line: "I missed the 2 to 4 window. I can be there Thursday at 8 a.m. If that fails you, say so and I will release the visit."

The study names the miss, offers a time, and leaves a way out. It does not decorate the delay.

Answer like a person. The public line has to match the sales note and the support reply, or you are running two voices.
