A voice you can say out loud.

Social media marketing, in this practice, is a voice and a posting habit. It is not a stack of lines with no one responsible for them.

Who may speak

One person owns the voice. Other people may draft. The owner reads every line aloud before it goes out. If they stumble, the line is wrong. A committee can catch a factual error. It should not sand the sentence until any company could have posted it.

One owner. Drafts are welcome. The last read is aloud.

A cadence you can keep

Pick a number you have already hit in a real week. If the true number is one honest post, the practice is one. A calendar full of empty days is a quieter lie than a fake countdown. Say what you will post, and say what you will leave unposted: a customer's private problem, a joke that needs the room, a promise the desk cannot keep.

What belongs in public is specific. Hours, a change, a delay, a straight description of the offer. Mood, by itself, is not a post.

The practice is the part you can repeat on a tired Thursday.

The same voice, later

The public line has to survive contact with sales and with support. If the post is warm and the reply is a form letter, you have two companies. Write the voice down in a few sentences a new hire can use: words you say, words you do not say, and the reply time you publish.

Write the voice so a new person can keep it.

Voice study. Not a client post. No brand names.

Situation. A neighborhood repair shop wants to tell people the Saturday hours for June.

The stiff draft: "We are glad to share that our doors will be open for your weekend needs."

The study line: "Saturday hours in June are 9 to 1. If you need a part before that, call the shop."

What changed. The hours are specific. The person at the counter could say the sentence. Nothing pretends to be urgent.

Voice study. Not a client post. No brand names.

Situation. A mail-order seed seller shipped Friday's orders on Tuesday.

The study line: "Orders placed Friday went out Tuesday, two days late. If yours is still missing, reply with the order number and we will trace it today."

The study keeps a fact, a delay, and a next step. It does not decorate the miss.
