Cue 02TuesdayOne public note

One note, or none.

Direction The cadence is a number you have already hit. If the true number this week is one, post one. A blank day is cleaner than a line you do not believe.

Voice study, not a client post A hardware counter wants to name Saturday hours. Study line: "Saturday hours in June are 9 to 1. Call before Friday if you need a special order." No brand. The line has a fact and a limit. It does not cheer.

Leave unposted A customer's private complaint. A joke that needs the room. A promise the desk cannot keep on Friday.
