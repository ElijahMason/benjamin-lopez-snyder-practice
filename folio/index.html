A week of public talk.

A practice order for social media marketing, sales, and customer support. It is a desk copy, not a log of a week that aired, and not a screen of counts. Durations are attention, not a promise that a clock ran.

  1. 01 15 min

    Monday, voice pass

    Read yesterday's public lines aloud. Cut any line you would not say at a counter.

  2. 02 20 min

    Tuesday, one public note

    One post, specific. If it needs a committee to sound like you, it waits.

  3. 03 30 min

    Wednesday, offer check

    Two sales conversations. Say the offer before you ask for anything.

  4. 04 20 min

    Thursday, queue

    Every open thread has an owner. Name what can wait for the published window.

  5. 05 15 min

    Friday, recovery

    If you were late or wrong, say it in the first line. Give a time you can keep.

  6. 06 None

    Saturday, leave it

    Do not invent a post to fill the day. Silence is a decision.

  7. 07 10 min

    Sunday, read-back

    Someone outside the company reads the week and tells you what they think you meant.
