04 / Support

Answer like a person.

People repeat what happened to them.

Customer support is part of the public reputation. The queue, the tone, and the recovery are the part people retell.

The queue

Write down what is waiting. Use two speeds, and only two: needs a person today, and can wait for the published window. Every thread has an owner. If the owner is out, the queue shows the name of the person who picked it up. An orphan thread is how a company sounds careless.

The window is the same promise you would print. If you tell customers you answer within two business days, the queue has to make that true. A prettier help page does not change a late reply.

Two speeds. An owner on every thread.

The tone

Short sentences. The same vocabulary you use in public. If you were wrong, say so in the first two lines. Do not open with a paragraph of sympathy that delays the fact. A reply that could belong to any company is a reply with the voice sanded off.

The first line is the fact.

The recovery

Name what happened. Name what you will do. Name the time. Then do it. If the time moves, you write first. The customer should not have to ask whether you forgot.

Recovery is not a new voice. It is the public voice under pressure, with fewer adjectives and a clock you can keep.

Fact, action, time. Then the action.

Support study. Not a real ticket. No brand names.

Situation. A chair arrived with a bent leg. The buyer wrote twice.

The weak reply: "We are sorry for any inconvenience, and we appreciate your patience."

The study reply: "The leg should not have shipped bent. I am sending a replacement leg today, and a label for the bent one. You will have a tracking number before 4 p.m. If it misses that, I will write to you before 4, not after."

The study keeps a fact, an action, and a time the sender can meet. The apology is the action, not a warm-up.
