Cue 03WednesdayOffer check

Say the offer before you ask.

Direction Two conversations. In each one, the buyer can leave. You are checking the offer, not closing a trap.

Spoken Repeat the buyer's problem in the buyer's words. Wait. If they correct you, start again. Then name the task, the term, the fee, and the limit. Send those same words afterward so nobody has to translate you.

Direction Strike any sentence that invents a deadline. A date counts when you would tell a friend the same date. A mood does not count.

Offer study, not a proposal "For four weeks I edit the public lines and sit in on two calls a week. You keep the voice when I stop. I do not buy ads. The fee is written here. You can say no."

There is no rate card on this desk. The fee changes with the volume of talk, and it still belongs in the first note.
